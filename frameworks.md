# Frameworks & checklists

Models you can reuse, taken from the articles.

## Aaker's 5Bs ([note](topics/02-brand-strategy/aaker-5bs-framework.md))
**Brand equity** sits at the centre. **Relevance**, **image** and **loyalty** drive it. The **portfolio** supports all three.

## Kraft Heinz: building a creative muscle ([note](topics/03-culture-and-ip/kraft-heinz-culture.md))
1. Start with three truths: brand, cultural and consumer. Build the idea from the tension between them.
2. Lean into earned and organic social.
3. Treat agencies as collaborative co-conspirators.
4. Always listen, and stay obsessed with the consumer.
5. Treat "no" as a request for more information.

## Goodles' maximalist rules ([note](topics/02-brand-strategy/goodles-maximalist.md))
Be known for colour · memorable names · maximum viable product · be the life of the party · brand on parade everywhere · build fans, not targets · manage the brand as if it's your last.

## Hedonic vs utilitarian scarcity ([note](topics/01-consumer-behaviour/blind-boxes-scarcity.md))
| | Supply scarcity | Demand scarcity |
|---|---|---|
| Hedonic | ✅ Strongest (emotional arousal) | – |
| Utilitarian | Weaker | ✅ Drives impulse buying (urgency) |

## Kantar: four ideas on IP ([note](topics/03-culture-and-ip/labubu-meaning.md))
1. Designer-toy IPs have broad general appeal.
2. Diverse aesthetics carry emotional value.
3. IP collaborations need a long-term strategy.
4. Co-branding must work both ways.

Plus: "the art of leaving blank space" means telling universal stories.

## Luma Research: four ad-testing criteria ([note](topics/04-representation/disability-in-ads-australia.md))
**Attention** · **Brand link** · **Takeout** · **Bonding**

## Yili: three uses of AI ([note](topics/06-china-tech-ai/yili-ai-marketing.md))
Instant insights → instant ideas → instant marketing (test what works). AI is the left brain; marketers are the right brain.

## Marcus Collins: engaging communities ([note](topics/01-consumer-behaviour/gen-z-cd-players.md))
Treat culture as a place to give, not a place to take. Contribute to the community's own characteristics.

## Closing the brand promise gap ([note](topics/02-brand-strategy/sephora-customer-experience.md))
Counter "enshittification" through emotional signals: **trust, respect, reassurance, care**.

## Tier 4 media mix ([note](topics/05-india-market/tier-4-markets.md))
Tier 1–2 markets are digital-first. Tier 4 markets need TV, radio and local activations, plus affordable trial packs and use-case education.

# China's smartphone market goes premium

## TL;DR
Chinese smartphone brands are overtaking Western rivals on innovation and premium features. Honor's **Magic V5** – billed as the thinnest, lightest foldable, with a built-in AI agent – is one example.

## What's happening
- Honor (formerly Huawei's budget arm) launched the Magic V5; its AI agent can generate presentations and coordinate ride-hailing.
- China foldable shipments: **2.84m in Q1**, **+53% YoY** (IDC via SCMP).
- Foldable category grew **27%** last year (Counterpoint via Fortune).
- **Two in three** foldables are sold in China; in the US/Europe foldables are ~**1%** of smartphone sales.
- Western new-phone sales have "stagnated" (Wired); Apple – back to growth in China – won't launch a foldable until ~2026 and is reportedly struggling to match local designs.

## Why it matters
- Global smartphone sales returned to growth, driven by top-end features and **premiumisation**.
- Chinese brands invested across innovation and supply chain – shifting from many cheap phones to fewer high-value ones.
- Honor's foldable: ~**$139m** investment to date; price **$1,000+**.
- Strong brands command a price premium – on average **14% higher** than competitors (WARC).
- Apple keeps a global advantage via brand equity; Chinese brands can counter by communicating innovation leadership, design and features.

# How Yili uses AI to give power to consumers

## TL;DR
Chinese dairy giant Yili (reaches **1.5bn consumers/year**) argues traditional long-term campaign planning is failing; AI enables a shift to **real-time action**, with power moving to consumers who decide instantly.

## The challenge
- A Chinese consumer may see up to **10,000 ads a day**.
- A commercial once took six months to make; on TikTok you have **two seconds** before the swipe.

## Three ways Yili uses AI
1. **Instant insights** – what consumers are talking about and interested in.
2. **Instant ideas** – executed quickly.
3. **Instant marketing** – push ideas to market and test which work, using research.

## Examples
- A video with four football stars from five national teams, made in **one month**, based on AI insights.
- Collaboration with **IShowSpeed** (top US YouTuber) for a Chinese national holiday video – created in **two weeks**.

## Friend, not enemy
- AI won't replace marketers; they are complementary.
- **AI = left brain** (data and logic). **Marketers = right brain** – they know the brand best.
- Zhang's closing idea (paraphrased): AI handles data, CMOs own humanity – the two complete each other.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Re-organise for speed: insight → idea → test loops in weeks, not months.
- Keep human ownership of brand meaning and judgement.

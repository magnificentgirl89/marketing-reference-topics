# Sephora's 56-year secret – customer experience

## TL;DR
In the most competitive beauty market ever, LVMH-owned Sephora stays on top not by innovating or opening more stores, but by obsessing over **customer experience**.

## Key facts
- 56 years old, French, LVMH-owned; ~**3,000 stores** worldwide, ~600 in the US; sells ~300 upscale beauty brands.
- Revenue **$18.5bn**; a "bright spot" in the LVMH portfolio (WSJ).
- Global beauty industry ~**$450bn**; Amazon controls **47%** of online beauty & personal care; Sephora has **9%**.

## Tactics
- In-store experience first – roughly two-thirds of sales come from stores; every square inch must be productive (redesigned Paris flagship).
- **Hulu partnership**: first long-form content series *Faces of Music* (Becky G, Victoria Monét, Chappell Roan).
- **WNBA partnership**: exclusive digital content, beauty activations, kiosks at US stadiums.
- New US initiative: earn directly from **influencer recommendations**, cutting out affiliates.
- Doubling down on booming categories like **fragrance**, which also appeals to teen boys.

## Why customer experience matters
- Differentiates against large, anonymous competitors (Amazon wins on price; experience is forgettable).
- WARC flags **"enshittification"** – erosion of value and trust as quality declines – as a defining marketing challenge.
- Fix: close the gap between brand promise and lived experience by focusing on emotional signals – **trust, respect, reassurance, care**.

## Key idea (paraphrased)
Sephora's CEO Guillaume Motte: Gen Z loves stores *and* the experience – boring retail is dead, attractive retail is alive.

# Consumer engagement grows at On

## TL;DR
Swiss sportswear brand On has moved beyond early-adopter runners to a broad audience, with buyers adding **apparel** to footwear – positioning as a premium challenger to Nike and adidas.

## What's happening
- **Expanding reach** – resonating well beyond runners; over-indexing with **Gen Z**.
- **Brand awareness** – fastest-growing in its category; US awareness **more than doubled in a year**. Helped by Zendaya, FKA Twigs, and ambassador **Iga Świątek** at Wimbledon.
- **Diverse portfolio** – nine footwear franchises each contributing **>5%** of revenue; expanding into tennis and fast into apparel.
- **Deepening engagement** – healthy repeat transactions; first- and second-time buyers increasingly add apparel to the basket.

## Why it matters
- Nike and adidas have had difficult moments; On has emerged as a credible challenger.
- Like them, On is moving into fashion/lifestyle – but on a premium, distinctive route.
- Co-founder David Allemann (paraphrased): don't expect On to swim in a sea of sameness – it will stay highly elevated, premium and distinctive.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Celebrity + athlete ambassadors can rapidly compound awareness.
- Franchise diversification reduces dependence on a single hero product.
- Cross-category baskets (footwear → apparel) signal deepening brand trust.

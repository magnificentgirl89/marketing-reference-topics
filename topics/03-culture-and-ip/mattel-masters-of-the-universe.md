# Mattel applies the Barbie playbook to Masters of the Universe

## TL;DR
Mattel aims to repeat the 2023 *Barbie* film's "cultural moment" with a live-action *Masters of the Universe* (MotU) film, due spring 2026, partnering with Amazon MGM Studios.

## Creating a cultural moment
- *Barbie* was the culmination of a long brand-reinvigoration process that gave the toy a voice in culture and sparked the fan-driven "Barbiecore" movement.
- MotU (40+ years old) never went away – fans keep it alive via memes, social media and conventions.
- Stats: a He-Man figure sells **every two minutes**; MotU gets a social mention **every 26 seconds**.
- Timing chosen to ride the **nostalgia** trend.
- Story designed to feel like "coming home" for existing fans while offering discovery of Eternia for newcomers.

## How it happens
- Trailer due in December; licensing partners engaged much earlier to ensure merchandise availability and quality – toys (incl. collector editions), fashion, digital gaming, publishing.
- **Multi-generational audience**: original fans plus their children → many touchpoints.
- Mattel builds platforms for partners to shine and co-create the moment.
- Amazon MGM brings its owned network of businesses to amplify widely.

## The big idea
Use a film to re-insert the property into popular culture and build a **long-term franchise** that keeps attracting fans for years.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Cultural moments are engineered: long lead times, aligned licensing ecosystems, partner platforms.
- Nostalgia works best when it also welcomes newcomers.

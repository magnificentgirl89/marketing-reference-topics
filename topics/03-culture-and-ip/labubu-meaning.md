# Labubu: what does that mean?

## TL;DR
Labubu dolls (from the 2015 picture book *The Monsters*) are one of China's biggest global hits. Kantar situates the craze within **emotional purchasing**, the rising importance of **IP**, and the **"ugly-cute" aesthetic**.

## Why Labubus matter
Many success factors at once: scarcity, celebrity endorsements, social virality, collectibility, limited collections, a huge secondary market. Initial testing ended in 2023; global breakthrough in 2024 – described as "gradual", a sign of the speed of Asian business.

## Key facts (Pop Mart)
- Q1 2025 revenue **+170% YoY**; China **+100%**, overseas **+480%**.
- HKSE filing: expected H1 revenue **+200%**, profit **+350%**.
- Shares still dipped ~6% – investors doubt growth will last.

## Big idea: "the art of leaving blank space" (留白的艺术)
To become global, tell **universal stories** instead of insisting on your own culture – leave room for consumers to project their own meanings.

## Kantar's four ideas
1. **High general appeal of designer toy IPs** – no longer niche; visual appeal regardless of backstory.
2. **Diverse aesthetics and emotional value** – happiness, relaxation, social connection → goodwill.
3. **IP collaboration needs a long-term strategy** – opportunistic tie-ins don't build sustainable growth.
4. **Co-branding should be two-way** – mutual benefit, not one brand taking from the IP.

# Kraft Heinz leans into culture to create marketing wins

## TL;DR
Kraft Heinz doesn't have an awareness or distribution problem – it has a **relevance** problem. CMO Todd Kaplan's answer: hijack cultural moments and invite culture into the conversation.

## Context
- **70 brands** across **50 categories**; **96%** household penetration.
- Consumers are overwhelmed by media; a message delivered isn't necessarily received.

## Campaigns
**Heinz × Mustard (Dijon McFarlane, music producer)**
- 1bn earned impressions; 3.2m social engagements
- Limited-edition mustard tested by Mustard
- Super Bowl ad + a parody on *Saturday Night Live*

**Oscar Mayer "Wiener 500" (Memorial Day / Indy 500)**
- Race between hot-dog-shaped cars
- 1.3m livestream viewers; 6.3bn impressions; #2 trending hashtag on X

## How to build a creative muscle like Heinz
1. **Start with three truths** – brand truth, cultural truth, consumer truth; build an idea from the tension between them.
2. **Lean into earned and organic social** – e.g. A1 sauce responded with a meme when the US education secretary confused "A1" with AI.
3. **Create a team of collaborative co-conspirators** – flatten client/agency formality to make creativity iterative.
4. **Always be listening, be consumer-obsessed** – limited Easter Jet-Puffed marshmallows in an egg crate, reacting to soaring egg prices; sold out in under a minute.
5. **Treat "no" as a request for more information** – persist when breaking the mould.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Relevance > reach for mature, high-penetration brands.
- Speed and permission to act quickly on cultural moments are organisational capabilities.

# Pet wellness becomes a premium "lifestyle partner" market

## TL;DR
Human wellness trends (supplements, organic, anxiety remedies, personalisation) are being transferred to pets. Owners increasingly see pets as "lifestyle partners" and pay a premium for products they believe are healthier.

## Key facts
- Global pet industry: roughly **$207bn**; US and China are the largest markets.
- **85%** of people believe "proper" nutrition and supplements matter as much for pets as for humans; **77%** of US pet owners will pay more for perceived healthier products (ADM).
- China: cat food overtook dog food, passing **200bn yuan (~$28m as reported)**; in 2024, sales of premium cat food priced above 50 yuan/kg rose **42% YoY** (WARC).

## Trends driving it
| Trend | Example |
|---|---|
| Trend extensions | Ashwagandha for anxiety, "collagen peptides" for joints – dubbed the "Goopification" of pet care |
| Personalisation | Tailored, innovative products borrowed from the beauty industry |
| New beliefs | Nutrition and supplements seen as equally important for pets and people |

## Risks / the downside
- Owners project their own health anxieties onto pets – linked to **vaccine scepticism** and distrust of vets.
- A vet quoted by the NYT warns well-meaning owners may not realise they could be endangering their animals.
- Claims must be backed by science: brands caught out in a crisis will struggle to defend themselves.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Wellness positioning commands premium pricing, but **substantiate claims**.
- Emotional storytelling works (e.g. Chinese brands' viral "pet hot pot" with beef and wild red shrimp).
- Pet brands that address longevity and overall wellness are expected to gain competitive edge (ADM).

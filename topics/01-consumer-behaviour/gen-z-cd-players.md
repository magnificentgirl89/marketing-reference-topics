# Why Gen Z wants CD players for Christmas

## TL;DR
Gen Z – who never owned CDs the first time – are adding CD players to wish-lists. It's less about audio and more about **belonging, nostalgia and escape from "doomscroll fatigue"**.

## Key facts
- Gen Z (18–24) is the major force behind sales of physical music (Vinyl Alliance).
- Survey of 2,500 vinyl fans: **76%** of Gen Z shop for records at least monthly; **80%** own a record player.
- **84%** shop for records in-store; **57%** want vinyl community events.
- **76%** cite owning a physical copy as the reason they collect.
- CD sales overall still decline, but CD players with Bluetooth show "resilience and growth".
- McCann estimates the "escape economy" will be worth **$13.9tr by 2028**.

## Why it matters
- Young people are looking for their **tribe**: comparing Spotify lists doesn't build identity the way record stores once did.
- Same pattern seen with Labubus and running clubs – communities around any interest.

## Culture as an influence (Marcus Collins)
- The larger force is the **"influential sway of culture"**.
- Engaging with communities should be about *giving*, not *taking*: contribute to the culture's characteristics in a way that feels authentic to members.

## Takeaways for marketers
- Nostalgia + community = opportunity for product innovation (e.g. modernised retro hardware).
- Earn your place in communities (e.g. Reddit) before trying to sell into them.

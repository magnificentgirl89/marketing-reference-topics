# SBI YONO – "You Only Need One"

## TL;DR
State Bank of India's YONO is an integrated **banking, investments and shopping** platform – a super-app approach to digital banking.

## Key facts
- **60m+ users**, **10m+ logins** and **500k+ transactions daily** – in under seven years since launch.
- Access to **100+ e-commerce websites**; book tickets, pay bills, invest, transfer funds.
- Features: 24×7 online banking, instant account opening, quick loans, card-less cash withdrawal, spend analyser.
- FY2021: **2m new accounts**, loans of **₹5,000+ crore** sourced.
- Extended to **seven foreign markets**; **40,000** customers onboarded abroad.
- New verticals: **YONO Agri** (farmers) and **YONO Business** (corporates & SMEs – letters of credit, cash management, supply-chain finance, forex).
- Uses accumulated transaction data to **sanction pre-approved loans**.

## Why it matters
- Shows a legacy public-sector bank building a platform ecosystem rather than a single-purpose app.
- Data from everyday transactions becomes a lending and cross-sell engine.

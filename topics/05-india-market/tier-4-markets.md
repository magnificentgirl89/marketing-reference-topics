# Brands eye India's Tier 4 markets

## TL;DR
Small towns and semi-rural areas (population **<50,000**) are individually small but collectively significant – and brands are shifting ad budgets and media strategies to reach them.

## What's happening
- Rising incomes, internet penetration and aspirational consumers make Tier 4 attractive.
- These markets grew **10%** between 2018 and 2023 (NielsenIQ via E4M).
- Meesho: **80%** of users come from Tier 2 and beyond; Tier 4 shoppers repeat-purchase most often.
- Agencies see brands allocating a focused share of ad budgets to these areas (Uday Mohan, COO, Havas Media India).
- Emerging themes:
  - Direct consumer engagement in local markets
  - Sharing use cases in new categories
  - Affordable products/pack sizes to aid trial

## What it means: media mix differs
| Tier 1 & 2 | Tier 4 |
|---|---|
| Digital dominates | **TV, radio and local activations** |

The key is a balanced, cost-effective approach that maximises reach while matching regional preferences (Mohan, paraphrased).

## Key idea (paraphrased)
Dabur India's Vaibhav Rathi: emerging tiers are the obvious next markets for categories that have been urban-centric.

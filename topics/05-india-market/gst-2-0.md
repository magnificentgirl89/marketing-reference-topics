# GST 2.0 brings early festive relief for Indian consumers

## TL;DR
India's GST overhaul consolidates four tax bands into **two – 5% and 18%** – easing household budgets just before the festive season, when purchasing confidence matters most.

## What's happening
- Billed as the most significant indirect-tax reform since GST launched in **2017**.
- FMCG brands and appliance makers already hinting at price corrections.

## Who gains / loses
| Category | Change |
|---|---|
| Daily staples (UHT milk, paneer/cottage cheese) | Fully **exempt** |
| Packaged foods (biscuits, noodles, chocolates) | **5%** (from 12–18%) |
| Personal care (hair oil, toothpaste, shampoo, dental floss) | **18% → 5%** |
| Health insurance premiums | **Exempt** (from 18%) |
| Consumer durables, small cars, two-wheelers (≤350cc), tyres | **Cheaper** |
| Luxury: SUVs, private aircraft, yachts, large cars | **More expensive** |

## Why it matters for marketers
- More than price corrections: improved **consumer sentiment** right as festive season peaks.
- Brands should capitalise on the optimism – communicate savings, time launches and promotions.

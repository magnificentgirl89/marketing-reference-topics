# IPL 2025 the best yet, says JioStar

## TL;DR
The TATA IPL 2025 broke records for reach, immersion and monetisation, blurring lines between sport, storytelling and shared experience.

## Key stats
- Total reach **1.19bn** – 537m TV + 652m digital.
- **235m** reach on Connected TV; **417m** on mobile.
- Final: **426m** viewers (189m TV, 237m digital).
- **47%** of IPL viewers on Star Sports were **women**.
- JioHotstar: **300m** subscribers; **1.04bn** Android app downloads.
- Regional-language feed growth: Hindi **+31%**, Telugu **+87%**, Tamil **+52%**.
- Multi-camera feeds and AI-powered match highlights.
- **425+ advertisers**, incl. **270+ debutants** across **40** categories.

## Why it matters
- The IPL is India's biggest sporting event – lessons for media owners worldwide.
- WARC Awards for Effectiveness case study covers Disney-owned Hotstar's growth strategy pre-merger with Reliance (forming JioStar).
- JioStar (Sanjog Gupta, CEO Sports & Live Experiences) says it was the most monetised edition, and the most monetised sporting event in India across ads and subscriptions.

## Takeaways for marketers
- CTV is now a mass channel for live sport in India.
- Large female audience – rethink "male-only" sports targeting.
- Regional language feeds are a growth lever.
